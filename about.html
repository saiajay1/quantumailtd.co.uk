About us

Two waves,onestronger signal.

The pattern at the top of our site is wave interference: two sources combining into something stronger than either alone. That’s how we see AI working best, with your expertise and the technology working together.

Our story

Founded in London in 2025 to close the gap between AI hype and AI that works.

AI is now more capable and more accessible than ever. Yet most organisations still struggle to turn it into something that reliably does a job, can be trusted, and pays for itself.

We started Quantum AI to close that gap. We’re an applied AI company: we don’t do pure research, and we don’t stop at strategy decks. We help businesses decide where AI fits, build the solutions, and when it’s justified, train custom models they own outright.

We’re based in London and focused on the UK. We know the regulatory landscape our clients work in, and we design for it from the start rather than bolting it on at the end.

Our measure of success is simple: AI that’s still in use, and still useful, a year after we’ve gone.

What we believe

Four beliefs that shape every project.

Evidence over enthusiasm

We measure before we promise. If the numbers don’t support a model, we won’t ship it, however impressive the demo.

Smallest thing that works

The best solution is often the simplest. We climb the model ladder only when testing shows we need to.

Your independence

You own your models, code and data. We document everything so you’re never locked in, including to us.

People in charge

AI should make skilled people more effective, not take away their judgement. We design oversight into every system.
