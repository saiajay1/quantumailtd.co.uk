The model ladder

Use the simplest AI that solves the problem.

There are four ways to make AI work for your business, from simplest to most specialised. We start at the bottom and only climb when testing shows we need to.

Prompting

A capable model, given clear instructions.

We use leading hosted models such as Anthropic’s Claude, with carefully designed prompts, examples and output checks. It’s the fastest route to value and often all you need for drafting, summarising and classifying text.

Best forGeneral language tasks
You needClear examples of good output
Typical startDays to a few weeks

Retrieval-augmented generation

Answers drawn from your own documents.

The model searches your policies, contracts or knowledge base before it answers, and cites where each answer came from. Your data stays in your systems and updates are instant, with no retraining.

Best forInternal knowledge, support, compliance
You needDocuments we can index securely
Typical startA few weeks

Fine-tuning

A model that has learned how you work.

We adapt an existing model on your examples so it reliably follows your format, terminology and judgement. Fine-tuning a smaller model can also cut costs and response times compared with a large general model.

Best forConsistent, high-volume, specialised tasks
You needHundreds to thousands of good examples
Typical startWeeks to a couple of months

Custom models

Built for one job, owned by you.

For specialised data, strict privacy or devices without a connection, we build compact models, for example by distilling a large model’s skill into a small one. You get the weights, an evaluation report and a model card.

Best forPrivacy, offline use, scale economics
You needA clear task and a solid evaluation set
Typical startOne to three months
